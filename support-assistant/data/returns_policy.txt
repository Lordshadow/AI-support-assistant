NovaRetail Store Return, Replacement and Refund Policy (v1.0)
Effective Date: 2026-01-01

Policy Clause 1.0 (Return Window): Physical items can be returned within 30 days of confirmed delivery if unused, unaltered, and retained in original manufacturer packaging with all accessories.

Policy Clause 2.0 (Refund Timelines): Once an eligible returned item is inspected at our fulfillment center, refunds take 3 to 5 business days to credit back to the customer's original payment method.

Policy Clause 3.0 (Damaged or Defective Items): If a product arrives physically damaged, shattered, or with verified hardware defects, NovaRetail provides an expedited free replacement or a prepaid return shipping label. Customers are never required to pay return shipping fees for defective or damaged items.

Policy Clause 6.0 (Digital Goods and Gift Cards): Digital goods, software licenses, gaming codes, and electronic gift cards are strictly non-refundable once delivered or redeemed. Autonomous support agents must never approve direct card refunds for digital vouchers; store credit or supervisor review may be offered for exceptional cases.

Policy Clause 7.0 (Shipping Address Modifications): Customers can request shipping address changes only within 2 hours of placing the order while the order status remains in 'Processing'. The customer must verify identity (registered account email and billing address) before any address update is finalized.
